# CIVIC ORDER

**A shift-work tragedy about a city that will not stop being an emergency.**

You are Warden Ilyas Renner, nine months into a job that keeps the lights on. You carry a
baton, a stamp, and a ration card for a family of three. You work Sector 4 — Kiln Row — a
damp grid of tenements, a canal, a laundry, four checkpoints, and eleven thousand people who
have learned to be very quiet.

Every day the Directorate hands you a quota. Every day you walk the Beat: inspect papers,
clear a room, disperse a crowd, or *don't*. Every night you file your report — and the report
is not a summary. **The report is what the city believes happened.** The Overseer reads it.
The insurgency reads the consequences. Your neighbours read the notices.

You are not a hero and you are not a monster. You are the part of the machine that touches
people.

---

## The three-layer fantasy

| Layer | Feeling | Reference feeling (not asset) |
|---|---|---|
| **The Beat** | walking a hostile street with too little authority and too much paperwork | *Half-Life 2*'s first hour: queues, loudspeakers, a city that is watching |
| **The Incident** | tense, lethal, legible room-clearing with people who have reasons | *Door Kickers* + *SWAT 4* |
| **The Desk** | the terrible freedom of the form: what do you write down? | *Papers, Please* + *Beholder* |

## The hook, in one sentence

**You don't choose what to do — you choose what gets recorded, and the city is built out of
records.**

## Pillars

1. **The Ledger Is the Weapon.** Bureaucracy is the primary interface to power.
2. **Force Is a Cost, Not a Verb.** Every escalation is logged, audited and paid for.
3. **The City Breathes.** A simulated sector with its own pressures; you are one input.
4. **Complicity, Not Villainy.** No cartoon evil. Tired professionals, rent, quotas.
5. **Everything Is on the Record.** Choices are remembered as *text*, and text can rot.

## Shape

- **Genre:** 2D top-down tactical sim / bureaucratic RPG. Real-time with pause, with a
  solo-only slow-motion combat mode.
- **Platform:** PC, browser-first (WebGL2), keyboard + mouse. Strictly single-player.
- **Session:** 25–40 min per shift. Campaign: 15 shifts (~9–12 h). Endless "Continuing
  Service" mode after.
- **Expansion:** *OVERSEER* — you stop being a body on the street and become the city's
  attention. The save you import is your own biased paperwork.

## What we are not making

Not a power fantasy about shooting civilians. Not a parody. Not a Half-Life mod, fan game, or
reskin — see `05-decisions/ADR-0003-original-ip.md` for the originality line, which is drawn
in specifics: our own ideology, our own iconography, our own vocabulary, and a mechanical
thesis (*the record is the world*) that Half-Life never had.
